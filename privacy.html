Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Atlantic Media ("we", "us") runs this website. This page explains what information we collect when you use it, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We keep it short on purpose; if anything is unclear, write to connect@atlanticmedia.in.

What we collect

  • Enquiries. When you send the form on our contact page we receive what you type: your name, phone number, email address, who you are (brand, creator, agency, applicant or other), the services you are interested in and any message you add.
  • Messages you send us directly by email, phone or WhatsApp, and anything you include in them.
  • Technical logs. Our hosting provider records standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested, time of request) to keep the site running and secure. We do not use these to identify you.

We do not run analytics or advertising trackers on this site, and we do not set cookies of our own.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and talk to you about working together.
  • To manage projects, partnerships and creator relationships once they start.
  • To keep the website secure and available.

We do not sell your information. We do not add you to marketing lists unless you ask us to.

Who else handles your data

  • Netlify hosts this site and stores form submissions so that we can read them.
  • Google provides the map on the contact page and the web fonts. Loading those may send your IP address to Google under Google's privacy policy.
  • Instagram and LinkedIn. Links to our profiles and to our creators' public profiles take you to those platforms, which have their own policies.

Creators shown on this site

The PRIOR globe shows creators we work with, using photos and public handles from their own social profiles, with their agreement. If you are one of them and want something changed or removed, email us and we will do it promptly.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for up to 24 months so we can follow up, then deleted unless we are working together. Information tied to an ongoing engagement is kept for as long as the engagement and our legal obligations require.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Email connect@atlanticmedia.in from the address you used, and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

This website is meant for businesses and adult creators. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be noted on the page itself.
